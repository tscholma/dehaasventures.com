Why AI Agent Companies Require a Different GTM Playbook

Vertrouwen verkopen
Selling Trust
Enterprise-klanten kopen geen AI-agent — ze kopen het vertrouwen dat autonomie veilig is. Dat vraagt om een compleet ander narratief dan klassieke SaaS: minder features, meer bewijs van controle, governance en menselijke verantwoordelijkheid.
Enterprise clients don't buy an AI agent — they buy confidence that autonomy is safe. That demands a fundamentally different narrative than classic SaaS: fewer feature claims, more proof of control, governance, and human accountability.
De juiste kamer binnenkomen
Getting in the Right Room
AI-agent-oplossingen raken meerdere stakeholders tegelijk: IT, Legal, Operations én de business. Zonder de juiste introductie op C-niveau strandt je pitch in de verkeerde werkgroep. Ons netwerk brengt je direct aan tafel bij de beslissers die er echt toe doen.
AI agent solutions touch multiple stakeholders simultaneously: IT, Legal, Operations, and the business. Without the right C-level introduction, your pitch gets stuck in the wrong working group. Our network takes you directly to the decision-makers who matter.
Adoptie als product
Adoption as Product

An agent that isn't used doesn't generate MRR. Human adoption is not an afterthought for AI agent companies — it is the core of your retention strategy. We build adoption mechanics in from day one of your commercial approach.
